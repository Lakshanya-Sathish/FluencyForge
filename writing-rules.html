Writing Rules

1. Independent Writing (Short Answers)

  • Write 80–120 words.
  • Give a clear opinion.
  • Add 1–2 examples or reasons.
  • Keep sentences simple and clear.

2. Opinion Essay (Full Essay)

  • Write 200–250 words.
  • Follow the introduction → body → conclusion format.
  • Give at least 2 strong reasons.
  • Use transitions like “firstly”, “in addition”, “therefore”.

3. Descriptive Writing

  • Focus on sensory details (sight, sound, smell, touch).
  • Use adjectives and vivid language.
  • Stay on one main idea.

4. Narrative Writing

  • Tell a clear story with a beginning, middle, and end.
  • Use past tense.
  • Include characters, setting, and conflict.
